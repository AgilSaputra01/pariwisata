# 22. UI/UX

Design direction:

```text
Modern
Tourism
Clean
Trustworthy
Friendly
Fast
Mobile First
```

Jangan menggunakan desain:

- template travel generik;
- terlalu banyak card;
- terlalu banyak gradient;
- stock-photo overload;
- dashboard-looking homepage;
- animasi yang mengganggu booking.

# 23. Homepage

Struktur:

```text
Header
↓
Hero
↓
Search / Booking Widget
↓
Popular Services
↓
Popular Routes
↓
Destinations
↓
Why Choose Us
↓
How Booking Works
↓
Reviews
↓
Travel Guide
↓
FAQ
↓
CTA
↓
Footer
```

Hero harus langsung menjawab:

**Apa yang ditawarkan?**

Contoh:

```text
Find Your Ride for Bali & Beyond

Car rental, private transport and fastboat
services for your journey.
```

CTA:

```text
Find Transport
Book Now
Chat on WhatsApp
```

# 24. Search & Discovery

User dapat mencari:

```text
Service
Route
Destination
Date
Passenger
```

Contoh:

```text
From:
Sanur

To:
Nusa Penida

Date:
20 September

Passengers:
2
```

Hasil:

```text
Available Fastboat
Schedule
Price
Duration
Operator
Book
```

---

# 25. Mobile First

Karena mayoritas wisatawan kemungkinan mencari transportasi menggunakan smartphone, mobile experience menjadi prioritas.

Wajib:

- sticky booking CTA;
- WhatsApp floating button;
- large touch targets;
- fast page loading;
- simplified forms;
- minimal popup;
- accessible navigation.
